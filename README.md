# Careers at The Campus App

The Campus App is the home of student experience and an ecosystem built around student life.

We are building the connective infrastructure that brings together academic life, wellbeing, community, and opportunity into one living system for students. Our product is already live, used by real students, and growing through university partnerships.

This repository exists to share open roles and provide clear information about opportunities to join the team.
